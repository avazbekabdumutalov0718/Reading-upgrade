## Vocabulary / Speaking Words
- Speaking collocations were expanded from 844 to exactly **850**.
- The section is now named **Speaking Words**.
- Fill the Gap and Sentence Builder no longer copy passage example sentences.
- They use deterministic, varied B1–B2 practice contexts created inside the app for each word/phrase.
- Sentence Builder now checks the exact generated sentence shown in that round.

## Speaking Upgrade
- “Speaking Part 1–3” was renamed **Speaking Upgrade**.
- Tab 1: the existing Part 1, Part 2 and Part 3 question bank, ideas, sample answers, collocations, timer, written answer and recording tools.
- Tab 2: the 30-topic Speaking collocation bank and topic wheel formerly exposed as MAX SPEAKING.
- Each Speaking topic uses 30 Speaking collocations in the 7 practice games.
- Examples shown in this section are now generated B1–B2 practice examples rather than the old repetitive generic examples.

## Writing Booster
- Writing-only topic collocations from the former combined bank were moved into Writing Booster as **Writing Topic Collocations**.
- It has a 30-topic selector, topic wheel and 7-game launch.
- The existing 50-day Writing Mastery, Idea Bank, Sample Answers, 15-card collocations, Writing Grammar and Writing Mock remain available.

## Mocks
- Added a unified **Mocks** hub for Speaking, Reading and Listening activity.
- Completed Speaking Mocks are archived.
- Speaking Mock PDF export includes the full questions and the learner's typed/transcribed answers.
- Imported Reading and Listening test pages send completion records back to the main app.
- Reading/Listening history records also retain the answered form values captured at submission time when available.

## Results / history

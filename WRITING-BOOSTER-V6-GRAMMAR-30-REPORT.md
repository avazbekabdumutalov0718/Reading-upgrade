# Writing Booster V6 — Grammar 30 Mixed Practice

- Grammar Book, Task 2 Grammar va Task 1 Grammar bo‘limlaridagi har bir mavzu/structure uchun aynan 30 ta mashq.
- Har topic yonida `30 ta mashq` tugmasi.
- Topic ochilganda avval English + Uzbek explanation, when/how va 3 ta tarjimali example ko‘rinadi.
- `30 ta turli mashqni boshlash` tugmasidan keyin alohida practice mode ochiladi.
- 10 xil mashq turi, har biridan 3 tadan: Qoidani tushunish, Misolni tanish, Gap Fill, So‘z tartibi, True/False, Uzbek→English, Rewrite, Combine, Academic Upgrade, O‘zingiz yozing.
- B2: 1–15; C1: 16–30.
- Har mashqda Tekshirish, feedback, model answer va Oldingi/Keyingi.
- 10 practice turiga tez sakrash tugmalari va 30-task progress mavjud.
- Oldingi g‘alati `Which grammar focus is demonstrated most clearly?` takroriy bloklari olib tashlandi.
